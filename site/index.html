How it works

GitHub introduces them, the Macs talk direct

Your Macs meet in a private GitHub repo of yours. The files never go there: they travel from one Mac to the other.

01

Any Mac sends, any Mac receives

Like a remote desktop app: pick the other Mac, then Send or Receive. It holds for that session only.

02

From one Mac to the next

01

Find

The Macs on your account show up as small laptops. Point at one to wake it, click to pick it.

02

Ask

Press Send or Receive. The other Mac asks its owner to approve.

03

The sender offers a tree of what it has; the receiver ticks what it wants.

04

Everything lands in place, then an AI agent of your choice wires up the rest.

Features

What makes the move painless

One small app on both Macs. Each Mac knows who it is, what it looks like and what it can offer.

EITHER WAY

Send or Receive, every time

No fixed sender. Any Mac sends to or takes from another, chosen per session.

PICK

A tree you tick

Categories, then apps and folders, then the parts of each app. Ticking a parent ticks everything under it.

DIRECT

Mac to Mac, not via a cloud

Files go over your network or Tailscale. GitHub only carries who is who and the sealed keys.

SEALED

End-to-end encrypted

Offers and keys are sealed to each Mac's own key; files stream under a key only the two Macs know.

AGENT

An agent finishes the job

Claude Code, Codex, OpenRouter, Ollama or your own command: it reads the brief and sets things up.

NATIVE

Small Rust app

One binary, drawn with Rune. Each Mac draws itself as the model and colour it really is.

In the repo

Only the handshake lives on GitHub.

you/macysync-bus/          # private
├── devices/
│   ├── 7f3a2c91.json    # name, look, public key
│   └── c04e19b7.json
├── requests/            # send or receive, to approve
└── transfers/
    └── 20261126-0830_7f3a2c91_c04e19b7/
        ├── offer.sealed # what the sender offers
        ├── picks.sealed # what the receiver takes
        └── key.sealed   # stream key and address

Never on GitHub

  • Your files: they go from Mac to Mac
  • Anything readable about what you move: offers and picks are sealed
  • Your GitHub sign in: it stays in the system keychain
  • Anything you leave unticked
