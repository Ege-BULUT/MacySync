Get Macy~Sync.

One app for every Mac, the old one and the new one.

macOSApple silicon · macOS 13+.zip ↓

Unzip it and move MacySync.app to Applications; it carries its own Tailscale for networks that keep Macs apart. Intel Macs have no build yet.

MacySync.app is not notarised, so macOS stops the first open: close the warning, then click Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/MacySync.app once.
